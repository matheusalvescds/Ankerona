### What is simulated

- The crewed mission and the crew (a tribute, not real people on Mars).
- The dose rates and warning time of the solar event.
- The values on the weather panel.
- The terrain, which is illustrative and not oriented north–south. The schematic crater map is.

## 02 · Where the name Ankerona comes from

Ankerona combines the first syllables of the names of the CHAPEA 1 crew, NASA's first mission that simulated a year of life on Mars.

AN·KE·RO·NA

ANca · KElly · ROss · NAthan

### The CHAPEA 1 mission

CHAPEA (*Crew Health and Performance Exploration Analog*) is a NASA program that studies how living on another world could affect a crew's health and performance.

| Item | Data |
|---|---|
| Start | June 25, 2023 |
| End | July 6, 2024 |
| Duration | 378 days |
| Location | Mars Dune Alpha, a 3D-printed habitat of about 1,700 sq ft (~160 m²) at NASA's Johnson Space Center, Houston |
| Simulated challenges | Isolation, limited resources, communication delays with Earth of up to 22 minutes each way, and simulated surface walks |

### From the real crew to the characters

| CHAPEA 1 crew member | Role in CHAPEA 1 | Ankerona character | Role in the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| **An**ca Selariu | Science officer; U.S. Navy microbiologist | **Ana Selari** | Geologist and protagonist |
| **Ke**lly Haston | Mission commander; research scientist | **Cmdr. Keli Hastor** | Commander |
| **Ro**ss Brockwell | Flight engineer; structural engineer | **Eng. Rossi Brook** | Engineer |
| **Na**than Jones | Medical officer; emergency physician | **Dr. Natan Jonas** | Physician |

The characters are not the real people. The names were adapted to honor the crew without portraying them, and the roles in the game closely follow the real ones. The protagonist is a woman, like the CHAPEA 1 science officer.

### Why honor CHAPEA

CHAPEA rehearses on Earth exactly what Ankerona shows in the game: a crew living in a habitat, managing limited resources and stepping out to walk on the surface of Mars. The tribute links the game's hypothetical mission to a real NASA effort to prepare humans for Mars.

### Sources

- NASA. *First Mars Crew Completes Yearlong Simulated Red Planet NASA Mission* (2024). nasa.gov/missions/analog-field-testing/chapea
- NASA. *Voyagers of Mars: The First CHAPEA Crew's Yearlong Journey*. nasa.gov/missions
- SpacePolicyOnline. *Four Analog Astronauts Complete 378-Day Practice Mars Mission* (2024).

## 03 · Characters

The crew of Ankerona's hypothetical mission. The names honor the CHAPEA 1 crew (see section 02).

#### Ana Selari

> Geologist · protagonist · tribute to Anca Selariu

The character the player controls. She leaves the habitat for the walk (EVA) across Gale Crater, studies the targets along the route and records each discovery in the Field Guide.

In dialogue she appears as **Dr. Selari**, with her portrait on the right of the dialogue box.

#### Cmdr. Keli Hastor

> Commander · tribute to Kelly Haston

Runs the mission. She gives the objectives, issues the alerts (such as the solar storm warning) and decides when to abort the walk.

In the game, she stays near the habitat.

#### Eng. Rossi Brook

> Engineer · tribute to Ross Brockwell

Looks after equipment and infrastructure. He stays by the pressurized rover, the mission's second shelter.

#### Dr. Natan Jonas

> Physician · tribute to Nathan Jones

Monitors the crew's health. During the solar event, he reports the dose received and what it means (for example, how many days on the surface it equals). He is the one who treats Ana when she returns to the habitat after an emergency.
The player can walk up to any crew member and press A to talk. During the solar event, the crew runs into the shelter together with Ana.

## 04 · Tatu, the guide rover

Tatu is the mission's cargo rover. It rolls ahead to show the way and brings together, in one place, the data Ana needs during the walk.

#### Who it is

A small robotic rover that accompanies the protagonist on the surface. In the game, the Commander introduces it like this: *"Tatu, our cargo rover, will lead the way. Follow Tatu: if you fall behind, it waits for you."*

"Tatu" is the common Brazilian Portuguese name for the armadillo.

### How it behaves

- **Leads the way:** it drives toward the mission's current target.
- **Avoids hazards:** when it can, it steers clear of sharp rocks and soft sand. That is the lesson from Curiosity's punctured wheels and from Spirit, which got stuck in soft soil.
- **Waits:** if Ana falls behind, it stops. After about 4 seconds, it drives back to her.
- **Rescues:** in an emergency (radiation dose above the limit, running out of O₂ or a damaged suit), it aborts the walk and takes Ana back to the habitat.

> The idea of Tatu leading the player was suggested by Jonatha, from the development team.

### Tatu's data panel

With the **TATU** button (T key), the player opens the panel with six functions:

| Function | What it shows | Real basis |
|---|---|---|
| Current target | Target briefing: what it is, why go, what to look for and the source | Mission data listed in section 01 |
| Field Guide | The entries recorded so far (up to 12) | See section 01 |
| Weather now | Air and ground temperature, pressure and UV index (simulated values) | REMS, Curiosity's weather station |
| Radiation | Today's dose and current rate, normal or high during the solar event | RAD, Curiosity's radiation detector |
| Hear the wind | The sound of the Martian wind | SuperCam microphone on Perseverance |
| Crater map | Schematic map of Gale Crater with the route targets | Gale Crater, Curiosity's work area since 2012 |

Tatu puts the core idea of the challenge into one character: bringing data from several NASA missions into a single view, to help someone on the surface plan and carry out a walk safely.
